Clipboard2Keystroke

Types your clipboard into web pages that block paste.

v1.0.0

How to use it

1
Copy the text
A password, a code, anything on your clipboard.
2
Click into the field
On the page where paste doesn’t work.
3
Click the icon
Or press the shortcut. It types for you.

Keyboard shortcut

Works even when a password field has focus.

Typing speed

Saved

Slow it down if a site drops characters. Instant types everything at once.

Try it

What the icon badge means

OKTyped into the field
?No text field was selected
STOPYou moved focus, so it stopped
ERRThis page can’t be typed into
Your clipboard is read only when you click the icon or press the shortcut. It is never stored or sent anywhere.
