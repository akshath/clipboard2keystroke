# Privacy policy

_Last updated: 1 November 2026_

This policy covers the Clipboard2Keystroke browser extension for Chrome and
Edge, and the Clipboard2Keystroke desktop script.

## In short

Clipboard2Keystroke does not collect, store, sell or send any of your data.
It has no servers, analytics or tracking, and it makes no network requests.

## What the extension reads, and when

- **Your clipboard.** The extension reads the text on your clipboard only at
  the moment you click its toolbar icon or press its keyboard shortcut. It
  types that text into the field you selected on the current page, then
  discards it. The text is never saved, logged or sent anywhere.
- **The current page.** When you trigger it, the extension looks at the
  current tab to find the field you selected and types into it. It does not
  read or keep any other page content, and it does nothing on pages until you
  trigger it.

## What the extension stores

- **Your typing speed setting** (a number of milliseconds), saved with the
  browser's extension storage. If you have browser sync turned on, your
  browser may sync this setting to your other devices through your browser
  account. Nothing else is stored.

## The desktop script

The desktop script runs only on your computer. It reads the clipboard when
you press its hotkey and types the text as keystrokes. It stores nothing and
makes no network requests.

## Third parties

No data is shared with anyone, because no data leaves your device.
